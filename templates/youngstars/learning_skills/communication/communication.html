💬 Communication Skills

Learn how to speak clearly, listen carefully, and express your ideas with confidence.

🗣️

Speak Clearly

Practice speaking slowly and clearly. Try explaining your favourite topic in your own words.

👂

Active Listening

Listen carefully when someone is speaking. Wait for your turn before answering.

📖

Storytelling

Read a short story and try telling it again using your own words.

🎤

Daily Practice

Spend a few minutes every day speaking, reading aloud or explaining a topic.
