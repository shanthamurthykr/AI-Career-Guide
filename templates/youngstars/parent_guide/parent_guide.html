Parent Guide

Helpful guidance for parents to support young learners, build healthy learning habits and encourage their children's curiosity and confidence.

📚

Support Learning

Help children create a comfortable and positive environment for learning.

  • Encourage regular study time.
  • Help them understand difficult topics.
  • Celebrate effort, not only marks.
🧠

Build Good Habits

Small daily habits can make learning easier and more enjoyable.

  • Encourage reading every day.
  • Maintain a healthy sleep routine.
  • Give time for breaks and hobbies.
💬

Communicate

Listening is one of the best ways parents can understand a young learner.

  • Ask about their interests.
  • Listen without immediately judging.
  • Talk about challenges calmly.
🎯

Set Small Goals

Help children turn big dreams into small, achievable steps.

  • Choose one goal at a time.
  • Track progress regularly.
  • Encourage them when progress is slow.
💻

Use Technology Wisely

Technology can be a powerful learning tool when used safely and responsibly.

  • Encourage educational use of technology.
  • Teach children to protect personal information.
  • Balance screen time with offline activities.
🌟

Build Confidence

Children learn better when they feel supported, respected and confident.

  • Appreciate their improvements.
  • Allow them to learn from mistakes.
  • Encourage questions and curiosity.

💡 Parent Tip of the Day

Instead of asking only, "How much did you score?", try asking, "What interesting thing did you learn today?"

A curious learner can grow far beyond a single test score. 🌱
